Fee and Settlement

This page explains what fees you may pay, how they are calculated, and what you can expect to receive.

Fee Terms

  • Service Fee: 0.5% of recovered value, minimum 1 USDC.
  • Withdrawal Fees: Hyperliquid charges 1 USDC per withdrawal.
  • Total Cost: Service Fee + Withdrawal Fees.
  • You Will Receive: Estimated Recoverable Value - Total Cost.

Estimated Recoverable Value means the assets that can actually be handled in this run. Assets that are still locked or waiting are not counted yet.

Fee Structure

Recovery usually includes two withdrawals, so Withdrawal Fees = 2 USDC:

  1. Service fee withdrawal: 1 USDC platform fee
  2. Final withdrawal: 1 USDC platform fee

Formulas

Service Fee = max(Estimated Recoverable Value x 0.5%, 1 USDC)
Withdrawal Fees = 2 USDC
Total Cost = Service Fee + Withdrawal Fees
You Will Receive = Estimated Recoverable Value - Total Cost

Examples

Estimated Recoverable Value Service Fee Withdrawal Fees Total Cost You Will Receive
100 USDC 1.00 2.00 3.00 97.00
1,000 USDC 5.00 2.00 7.00 993.00
10,000 USDC 50.00 2.00 52.00 9,948.00

What You See on the Result Page

  • Before execution: estimated values.
  • After execution: actual values.
  • Small differences may happen because prices can change during execution.

Notes

  • Address detection is always free.
  • If nothing is successfully handled, no unnecessary service fee is charged.
  • Final withdrawal must still meet Hyperliquid's minimum withdrawal requirement (commonly 1.01 USDC).
  • After withdrawal is submitted, arrival on Arbitrum depends on Hyperliquid processing speed and may take several to more than ten minutes.
