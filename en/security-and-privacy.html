Security and Privacy ​

This page explains how to use the bot more safely and what you can verify yourself.

Safe Use Basics ​

  • Use only the official bot entry.
  • Never provide your private key or mnemonic to any person, group, form, or website outside the bot's official recovery flow.
  • If you cannot confirm that you are using the official bot, stop and verify before continuing.
  • Never let private keys, mnemonics, or full signatures appear in screenshots or chat records.

What You Can Verify ​

  • Key steps provide transaction hashes or clear execution results.
  • You can verify transaction status and fund movement on-chain.
  • You can use the source address, received amount, and fees to check the result yourself.

Safe Operation Practices ​

  1. Use only the official bot entry: @HyperliquidRecoveryBot.
  2. Operate from a private device and network; avoid public Wi-Fi.
  3. Verify the source address before confirming (withdrawal settles to the same address on Arbitrum).
  4. Outside the bot's official recovery flow, never share, screenshot, forward, or paste private keys or mnemonics anywhere.

Risk Boundary ​

  • The bot cannot bypass platform rules such as lock periods, waiting periods, or minimum withdrawal limits.
  • If some assets are still waiting or locked, come back and run recovery again after the required time.
  • If the private key or mnemonic does not match the address, the bot will stop the process.
  • If anyone asks you to provide credentials outside the official bot flow, treat it as suspicious and stop.
